|------|---------|
| `SC_N_NOAPPL` | No SC existed + no harsh sentence → correct/default |
| `SC_N_APPL` | No SC existed + LWOP/Death/90+ given → ⚠️ overcharging |

| `SC_Y_APPL` | SC existed + LWOP/Death/90+ given → correct |

**First part (N/Y)** = does a §190.2 special circumstance exist in the facts?
**Second part (NOAPPL/APPL)** = was LWOP, death, or 90+ years sentenced?

You MUST understand the legal difference: ### GANG ENHANCEMENT (Penal Code 186.22(b)) **What it is:** - An ADDITIONAL punishment added to the base sentence - Does NOT change the underlying charge - Does NOT make the case eligible for LWOP or death penalty - Simply adds years (typically 5, 10, 15, or 25 years) on top of murder sentence **Legal standard:** - Prosecution must prove crime was "committed for the benefit of, at the direction of, or in association with a criminal street gang" - Lower burden than special circumstance **Effect on sentencing:** - Murder (25-to-life) + Gang Enhancement (10 years) = 35 years to life - Still eligible for parole after 35 years - **THIS IS NOT LWOP** **Keywords in articles:** - "gang enhancement" - "gang allegation" - "sentenced with gang enhancement" - "gang-related" - "plus X years for gang enhancement" ### GANG SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE (Penal Code 190.2(a)(22)) **What it is:** - A DISTINCT LEGAL FINDING that fundamentally changes the nature of the charge - Transforms standard murder into capital murder - Makes defendant eligible for LWOP or death penalty - Requires separate jury finding of "true" **Legal standard (MUCH HIGHER):** - Prosecution must prove the murder was **intentionally committed to further the activities of the gang** - Must show the killing itself was done **TO BENEFIT THE GANG** (not just that defendant was a gang member) - Requires proving specific intent related to gang benefit - Much more complex legal analysis than enhancement **Effect on sentencing:** - If found true: Eligible for LWOP or Death - If LWOP given: SC WAS APPLIED → _APPL - Completely different sentencing landscape **Keywords in articles:** - "gang special circumstance" - "special circumstance of gang murder" - "§190.2" or "190.2" with gang reference - "jury found the gang special circumstance true" - "special circumstance allegation" - "capital murder with gang special circumstance" ### COMPARISON TABLE | Aspect | Gang Enhancement | Gang Special Circumstance | |--------|------------------|---------------------------| | **Penal Code** | PC 186.22(b) | PC 190.2(a)(22) | | **What it does** | Adds years to sentence | Changes charge to capital murder | | **Burden of proof** | For benefit/association with gang | Killing done TO BENEFIT gang | | **Maximum sentence** | Life WITH parole possibility | LWOP or Death | | **Counts as SC?** | ❌ **NO** | ✅ **YES** | | **Common in articles** | Very common | Less common | ### DECISION RULE FOR GANG CASES ``` Is "special circumstance" explicitly mentioned with gang? │ ├─► NO → Assume gang ENHANCEMENT only → NO SC exists │ → If sentence < 90 years: SC_N_NOAPPL │ → If sentence = LWOP/90+: SC_N_APPL (overcharging flag!) │ └─► YES → Gang SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE exists → SC exists → If sentence < 90 years: SC_Y_NOAPPL (undercharging) → If sentence = LWOP/90+: SC_Y_APPL (correct) ``` ### IMPORTANT NOTE FOR CRITICAL ANALYSIS Whenever you encounter a gang-related case, you MUST note in the **Critical Analysis** field: - THE 4 CLASSIFICATION CODES - DETAILED ## 3.1 Code Definitions with Examples ### SC_N_NOAPPL (No SC Existed, None Applied) **Definition**: There were no §190.2 special circumstance factors present in the case facts, AND the defendant did NOT receive LWOP/death/90+ years. **This is the DEFAULT code** - use when unsure or insufficient information. **When to use:** - Single victim, no felony murder, no ambush, standard murder - Sentence is "X years to life" where X < 90 - Attempted murder (victim survived) - Manslaughter cases - Vehicular homicide **Example cases:** - Bar fight resulting in death → 15 years to life - Domestic dispute murder → 25 years to life - Drug deal gone wrong (single victim, no robbery) → 20 years to life ### SC_N_APPL (No SC Existed, But Was Applied) - OVERCHARGING **Definition**: There were NO §190.2 special circumstance factors present, BUT the defendant received LWOP/death/90+ years anyway. **This indicates potential OVERCHARGING** - important for equity analysis. **When to use:** - No SC factors identifiable in case facts - BUT sentence was LWOP, death, or 90+ years **Example:** - Simple argument-based murder, single victim, no felony → LWOP - This would be unusual and worth flagging ### SC_Y_NOAPPL (SC Existed, But Not Applied) - UNDERCHARGING **Definition**: §190.2 special circumstance factors WERE present in the case facts, BUT the defendant did NOT receive LWOP/death/90+ years. **This indicates potential UNDERCHARGING** - important for equity analysis. **When to use:** - Multiple victims killed → but only 25-to-life - Murder during robbery → but only 40 years - Gang special circumstance alleged → but 18 years to life - Lying in wait proven → but 50 years to life **Example cases:** - Double murder → 50 years to life (not LWOP) = SC_Y_NOAPPL - Murder during robbery → 25-to-life = SC_Y_NOAPPL - Gang murder with §190.2 allegation → 18-to-life = SC_Y_NOAPPL ### SC_Y_APPL (SC Existed and Was Applied) - CORRECT APPLICATION **Definition**: §190.2 special circumstance factors WERE present, AND the defendant received LWOP/death/90+ years. **This indicates CORRECT application of the law.** **When to use:** - Multiple victims + LWOP - Felony murder + LWOP - Lying in wait + death penalty - Any SC factor + 90+ years **Example cases:** - Double murder → LWOP = SC_Y_APPL - Murder during robbery → Life without parole = SC_Y_APPL - Torture murder → Death penalty = SC_Y_APPL ## 3.2 Decision Flowchart (Detailed) ``` START: Analyze case │ ├─► STEP 1: Is this a completed MURDER? │ │ │ ├─► NO (attempted murder, manslaughter, victim survived) │ │ └─► SC_N_NOAPPL (§190.2 doesn't apply to non-murders) │ │ │ └─► YES (victim died, murder charge) │ │ │ └─► STEP 2: Do ANY §190.2 factors exist? │ │ │ ├─► Check: Multiple victims? │ ├─► Check: Felony murder? (robbery/burglary/kidnap/rape/carjack) │ ├─► Check: Lying in wait / ambush? │ ├─► Check: Drive-by shooting? │ ├─► Check: Gang §190.2 special circumstance? │ ├─► Check: Special victim? (cop/firefighter/witness) │ ├─► Check: Torture? │ ├─► Check: Financial gain / murder for hire? │ ├─► Check: Prior murder conviction? │ │ │ ├─► NO SC factors found │ │ │ │ │ └─► STEP 3a: What was the sentence? │ │ │ │ │ ├─► LWOP / Death / 90+ years │ │ │ └─► SC_N_APPL (overcharging!) │ │ │ │ │ └─► Less than 90 years / X-to-life (X<90) │ │ └─► SC_N_NOAPPL (correct) │ │ │ └─► YES, SC factor(s) found │ │ │ └─► STEP 3b: What was the sentence? │ │ │ ├─► LWOP / Death / 90+ years │ │ └─► SC_Y_APPL (correct) │ │ │ └─► Less than 90 years / X-to-life (X<90) │ └─► SC_Y_NOAPPL (undercharging!) ``` ## 3.3 Quick Reference Table | SC Exists? | Sentence LWOP/Death/90+? | Code | Meaning | |------------|--------------------------|------|---------| | NO | NO | SC_N_NOAPPL | Correct - no SC, standard sentence | | NO | YES | SC_N_APPL | OVERCHARGING - harsh sentence without SC | | YES | NO | SC_Y_NOAPPL | UNDERCHARGING - SC exists but lenient sentence | | YES | YES | SC_Y_APPL | Correct - SC applied appropriately | --- # 4. SENTENCING RULES - COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE ## 4.1 What Counts as "SC Applied"? ### Sentences That = SC APPLIED (_APPL) | Sentence Language | Code | Reason | |-------------------|------|--------| | "Life without parole" | _APPL | LWOP | | "Life without the possibility of parole" | _APPL | LWOP | | "LWOP" | _APPL | LWOP | | "Death" | _APPL | Death penalty | | "Death penalty" | _APPL | Death penalty | | "Sentenced to death" | _APPL | Death penalty | | "100 years to life" | _APPL | 90+ years | | "95 years" | _APPL | 90+ years | | "90 years to life" | _APPL | Exactly 90 = APPL | | "Two life terms" (consecutive) | _APPL | Effectively LWOP | | "150 years to life" | _APPL | 90+ years | ### Sentences That = SC NOT APPLIED (_NOAPPL) | Sentence Language | Code | Reason | |-------------------|------|--------| | "25 years to life" | _NOAPPL | 25 < 90 | | "25 to life" | _NOAPPL | 25 < 90 | | "15-life" | _NOAPPL | 15 < 90 | | "18 years to life" | _NOAPPL | 18 < 90 | | "40 years to life" | _NOAPPL | 40 < 90 | | "50 years to life" | _NOAPPL | 50 < 90 | | "75 years to life" | _NOAPPL | 75 < 90 | | "89 years to life" | _NOAPPL | 89 < 90 | | "15 years" | _NOAPPL | Less than 90 | | "25 years" | _NOAPPL | Less than 90 | | "9 years state prison" | _NOAPPL | Less than 90 | ## 4.2 The 90-Year Rule **WHY 90 YEARS?** 90+ years effectively means the person will die in prison (no realistic possibility of release), which is functionally equivalent to LWOP. This indicates SC was applied even if not explicitly stated. **THE RULE:** - **90 or more years = _APPL** - **89 or fewer years = _NOAPPL** **Examples:** | Sentence | Years | Code | |----------|-------|------| | 90 years to life | 90 | _APPL | | 89 years to life | 89 | _NOAPPL | | 91 years | 91 | _APPL | | 50 years + 40 years consecutive | 90 | _APPL | | 50 years + 35 years consecutive | 85 | _NOAPPL | ## 4.3 How to Parse Complex Sentences ### Multiple Counts (Consecutive) Add them together: - "25 years to life on count 1, plus 25 years on count 2" = 50 years total = _NOAPPL - "50 years to life plus 50 years enhancement" = 100 years = _APPL ### Enhancements Include enhancements in total: - "25-to-life plus 25-to-life gun enhancement" = 50 years = _NOAPPL - "25-to-life plus 75-year enhancement" = 100 years = _APPL ### "Two Life Terms" Depends on consecutive vs concurrent: - "Two consecutive life terms" = Effectively LWOP = _APPL - "Two concurrent life terms" = Same as one life term = Check the minimum years ### Indeterminate Sentences For "X to life" sentences, use X: - "25 to life" → X = 25 → _NOAPPL - "100 to life" → X = 100 → _APPL ## 4.4 Sentence Pending / Unknown If sentence is pending, unknown, or case is ongoing: - Default to **SC_N_NOAPPL** - Set confidence to **low** or **medium** - Note "sentence pending" in summary - The SC code is based on what SHOULD apply, but without sentence we can't confirm application --- # 5. AGE CALCULATION - COMPLETE INSTRUCTIONS ## 5.1 Which Date to Use ### Priority Order: 1. **Crime Date / Incident Date** (BEST) - When the crime actually occurred 2. **Arrest Date** - When defendant was arrested 3. **Filing Date** - When charges were filed (LAST RESORT) ### Why This Matters: The research question is about defendant's age AT TIME OF CRIME, not at filing or sentencing. ## 5.2 Calculation Method ``` Age at Crime = Crime Year - Birth Year - Adjustment Where Adjustment = 1 if crime date is before birthday in that year, else 0 ``` ### Simple Calculation: ```python from datetime import datetime def calculate_age(dob, crime_date): age = crime_date.year - dob.year # Adjust if birthday hasn't occurred yet in crime year if (crime_date.month, crime_date.day) < (dob.month, dob.day): age -= 1 return age ``` ## 5.3 Handling Excel Serial Dates Excel stores dates as numbers (days since 1899-12-30): | Excel Serial | Actual Date | |--------------|-------------| | 44562 | January 1, 2022 | | 36526 | January 1, 2000 | | 32874 | January 1, 1990 | ### Conversion: ```python import pandas as pd def excel_to_date(serial): if isinstance(serial, (int, float)): return pd.Timestamp('1899-12-30') + pd.Timedelta(days=int(serial)) return pd.to_datetime(serial) ``` ## 5.4 Age Range Classification | Age | Range Code | Description | |-----|------------|-------------| | 0-17 | **Below 18** | Juvenile at time of crime | | 18-26 | **18-26** | Young adult / youth offender range | | 27+ | **Above 26** | Adult | | Unknown | **Unknown** | Could not determine age | ### Why These Ranges? - **Below 18**: Juvenile justice considerations, Miller v. Alabama implications - **18-26**: Brain development research, youthful offender policies - **Above 26**: Fully adult, standard sentencing ## 5.5 Critical Rule: Recalculate from Final Age **PROBLEM**: Sometimes DOB calculation gives one age, but research finds different age. **SOLUTION**: Always recalculate range from the FINAL age used: ```python def get_age_range(age): if age is None or age == "Unknown": return "Unknown" try: a = int(age) if a < 18: return "Below 18" elif 18 <= a <= 26: return "18-26" else: return "Above 26" except: return "Unknown" # CORRECT: final_age = research_found_age or calculated_age final_range = get_age_range(final_age) # WRONG: # Using calculated_range when research found different age ``` ## 5.6 When Research Finds Different Age | Source | Age Found | Action | |--------|-----------|--------| | DOB calculation | 30 | Start with this | | News article says | 22 | Use 22 (more specific to crime) | | Research says "in his 20s" | ~25 | Use calculated age (30) as more precise | **Rule**: Use the most specific, reliable age. News articles reporting "22-year-old defendant" at time of crime are often more accurate than DOB calculations. --- # 6.
